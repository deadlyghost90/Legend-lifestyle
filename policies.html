Shipping & returns

Before accepting orders, Legend Lifestyle should publish the locations it serves, shipping methods and charges, estimated delivery windows, tracking availability, cancellation rules, and a clear return or exchange process.

Customers should be told how to request support, the condition required for returned items, which products may be excluded where legally permitted, and when refunds are normally processed. No delivery promise or return entitlement is confirmed by this draft.

  • Delivery regions and fees: to be confirmed.
  • Dispatch and delivery estimates: to be confirmed.
  • Return window, eligibility and process: to be confirmed.
  • Refund method and expected timing: to be confirmed.

Privacy notice

A final privacy notice should explain what information the store collects, why it is needed, the lawful basis where applicable, how long it is retained, who receives it (including hosting, analytics, customer-support and payment providers), and how customers can exercise applicable privacy rights.

This prototype currently uses browser memory for the shopping-bag demo and does not submit a real order. Newsletter signup displays a message only; it is not connected to a mailing list. Before production, document the actual data flows and update this notice to match them.

Terms of service

Final terms should identify the legal business operating the store, provide accurate product descriptions and pricing rules, describe the order acceptance process, payment and delivery terms, warranties and limitations allowed by law, complaint handling, and dispute-resolution information.

Product cards on this prototype use demonstration names and prices. They are not an offer to sell real goods. Replace the sample catalogue and review the terms before enabling checkout.
